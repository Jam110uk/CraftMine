⚠ The Golden Rule: Never dig straight up or straight down.
Build spiral staircases instead — straight-down risks lava; straight-up risks falling gravel.

Strategies

Build spiral staircases — never dig straight up or straight down.
Mine at y=11 for best access to Diamond, Gold, Redstone, and Lapis all at once.
Bring a water bucket: pour it on lava to create obsidian and safely cross.
Emerald only spawns in mountain biomes — search there exclusively.
Bring a Blast Furnace for smelting ores twice as fast underground.
Place torches on the left wall going in — follow them on the right coming out.
A Bed underground lets you skip dangerous nights and reset your spawn point.
Status effects from potions (Haste, Night Vision) dramatically speed up mining.
🌤 The Overworld
Plains
The first biome ever added to Minecraft. Flat grass and dirt terrain with abundant farm animals and villages. Bees and ruined Nether portals can now spawn here. The easiest biome to gather food in due to seeds from grass and plentiful livestock.
🌾 Seeds & Wheat 🐄 Farm Animals 🏘 Villages 🐝 Bees Subtype: Sunflower Plains (1.8)
Forest
Mainly consists of trees, most commonly oak. A reliable source of wood — the most useful resource in the game — making this a great early-game biome.
🪵 Oak Wood Subtype: Birch Forest Subtype: Flower Forest (Bees spawn)
Dark Forest
Features dark oak trees and huge mushrooms. Very dangerous — dense canopy creates many dark spots where hostile mobs spawn even during the day. Woodland Mansions generate here, though rarely.
⚠ Mobs spawn in daylight 🍄 Giant Mushrooms 🏚 Woodland Mansion (rare)
Swamp
Shallow discolored water with oak trees draped in vines. The easiest biome to harvest clay in. The only natural source of lily pads and blue orchids. Witch huts and slimes spawn here.
🧱 Clay (abundant) 🪷 Lily Pads 🧙 Witch Huts 🟢 Slimes
Jungle
Extremely dense forest with the tallest trees of any biome, covered in vines. The only biome where ocelots and parrots spawn. Cocoa pods grow here, and wild melons can also be found. May contain a Jungle Temple.
🍈 Wild Melons 🌿 Cocoa Pods 🐆 Ocelots & Parrots only here 🏛 Jungle Temple (rare)
Taiga
Dense spruce forest with dull grey-blue grass and hilly terrain. Wolves are more frequent here. In colder variants, water freezes into ice, which can destroy sugar cane. Foxes were added in 1.14.
🌲 Spruce Wood 🐺 Wolves 🦊 Foxes (1.14+) Subtype: Snowy Taiga
Snowy Tundra
Completely covered in snow with frozen water. Snow falls instead of rain. Wolves can spawn here. Ice spikes plains is a rare subtype with large packed-ice formations and no trees.
❄ Always snowing 🐺 Wolves Subtype: Ice Spikes Plains (rare)
Mountains
Highly mountainous terrain with cliffs, peaks, valleys and overhangs. More underground cave systems than any other biome. Snow caps high peaks. Falling is a serious risk. The only biome where emerald ore spawns — along with dark forests.
💚 Emerald Ore (exclusive!) ⚠ Fall damage risk 🕳 Most cave systems
Desert
The most abundant source of cacti. Hills of sand and sandstone with naturally-occurring desert wells. Desert temples can spawn in large deserts. Villages replace wood with sandstone. It never rains in the desert.
🌵 Cacti (abundant) 🪨 Sandstone 🏛 Desert Temple 🏘 Sandstone Villages
Badlands (Mesa)
Features colourful clay and natural terracotta, red sand, dead bushes, and cacti. Rare — usually spawns near savanna. In Bedrock Edition, gold ore can be found at any level here, and abandoned mineshafts appear on the surface.
🟫 Terracotta & Clay 🔴 Red Sand Gold at any level (BE)
Savanna
Dry, dull grass with acacia trees. Villages and horses spawn naturally. Llamas also spawn here. It never rains in the savanna, and it often borders deserts and badlands.
🌳 Acacia Wood 🐴 Horses & Llamas 🏘 Villages ☀ Never rains
Ocean
Enormous biomes of open water, 10,000–25,000 blocks wide. Gravel, dirt, and clay form the floor. Can flood into abandoned mineshafts. Deep ocean variants can be 30 blocks deep and contain ocean monuments guarded by hostile guardians. Fish, dolphins, and turtles also spawn (1.13+).
🧱 Clay floor 🏛 Ocean Monument (deep) ⚔ Guardians ⚠ Can spawn without land nearby
Mushroom Fields
A rare biome found far from other landmasses. Mycelium replaces grass, and giant mushrooms grow instead of trees. The only habitat of mooshrooms. Remarkably, no hostile mobs spawn here at all.
✅ No hostile mobs! 🍄 Giant Mushrooms 🐄 Mooshrooms (exclusive)
🔥 Other Dimensions
The Nether
Reached only through an obsidian portal lit with Flint and Steel. Floor is netherrack (burns indefinitely), soul sand, magma blocks, and gravel pits. Water evaporates instantly. Beds explode when used. Compasses and maps are useless. Mobs: ghasts, zombified piglins, blazes, wither skeletons.
💥 Beds explode! 🌊 No water allowed ✨ Glowstone clusters 👻 Ghasts, Blazes 4 new biomes in 1.16
The End
Floating islands in a black void. Reached via End portal in a stronghold using 12 Eyes of Ender. Ground is End stone with obsidian pillars topped by Ender Crystals that heal the Ender Dragon. Defeating the Dragon "completes" the game and opens the return portal. Compasses, clocks, and beds do not work here.
🐉 Ender Dragon boss ⚠ One way in — kill the Dragon to leave 🏙 End Cities (outer End)

Mob Behaviour Tips

Zombies & Skeletons burn in sunlight (not in rain)
Don't look directly at Endermen — they'll attack!
Back away from Creepers before the fuse timer runs out
Witches throw poison potions — close the distance fast
Mobs spawn by biome — swamps have witches, mountains have Endermen
Endermen teleport when near death — watch out!
